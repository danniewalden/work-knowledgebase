## Finding 1 — Slug drift between `raw/` and `wiki/sources/` ⚠ the real issue

**27 raw captures are compiled under a source-page slug that differs from their filename.** Examples:

| raw file | wiki source page |
| --- | --- |
| `willison-what-is-agentic-engineering` | `willison-agentic-engineering-patterns` |
| `devadoss-cead-capability-aligned-enterprise-agent-design` | `devadoss-cead-capability-aligned-agent-design` |
| `dilger-flea-market-model-code-deploy-30min` | `dilger-flea-market-model-to-deploy` |
| `rico-fritzsche-event-sourcing-does-not-require-aggregates-ccc-vs-dcb` | `rico-fritzsche-es-does-not-require-aggregates-ccc-vs-dcb` |
| `roden-too-many-islands-too-few-bridges-em-conf-2026` | `roden-too-many-islands-em-conf-2026` |

Plus three-to-one cases (the Goeleven series page covers three raw articles) and two-to-one cases (one Fritzsche page covers a post and its restatement), which are *correct* editorial decisions — but they make the mapping non-recoverable by filename.

**Why it matters.** Nothing in the wiki breaks. But "is this raw file ingested yet?" is a question asked on every research sweep and every digest, and the only way to answer it mechanically is filename matching — which is wrong 27 times out of 191. **This morning's digest reported "~60 un-ingested" on that basis; the true figure is 46**, and the log's hand-maintained estimate of "~43" was closer than the automated check. Five consecutive sweep verdicts have described a backlog roughly 30% larger than it is.

**Recommended fix (needs your say-so, it's a schema change to `CLAUDE.md`):** add a `raw_file:` field to source-page frontmatter listing the raw capture(s) that page compiles —

```yaml
raw_file: [raw/articles/willison-what-is-agentic-engineering.md]
```

That makes the mapping explicit, survives deliberate renames, handles many-to-one pages correctly, and turns the backlog question into a one-line query. Backfilling the 27 existing drifts is mechanical.
