- **TLDR:** The most concrete build-out yet of his "feature factory." **3. Osmani — "Own the Outer Loop"** *(Addy Osmani, Elevate/Substack, 2026-07-09)* — `raw/articles/addyosmani-own-the-outer-loop` · filed, not yet ingested
- **TLDR:** The follow-on to his loop-engineering primary. As agents take the *inner* execution loop (investigate→implement→verify→repeat), engineers must own the *outer* loop — accountability — via three terms: **Quality** (back-pressure checks that produce evidence), **Verdict** (the human ship/block/redirect production decision), **Answerability** (the guarantee you can explain *why*). Backed with fresh data (Sonar 2026: 42% of committed code AI-generated; Anthropic RCT: AI-assisted engineers score 17pp lower on comprehension; Wharton: people accepted wrong AI answers ~75% of the time, with more confidence). "The agent can ship more than you can review" is the new bottleneck.
- **Why it's worth reading:** It sharpens the "stay the engineer" edge of your loop-engineering thread and pairs directly with the already-ingested Willison-DRI note — together they close the accountability corner of the loop.

**4. Dilger — "My Wife Asked Me to Build a Flea-Market Registration"** *(Martin Dilger, LinkedIn, 2026-07-01)* — `raw/notes/dilger-flea-market-model-code-deploy-30min` · filed, not yet ingested
- **TLDR:** A deliberately mundane worked example: modeling → building → deploying a real registration form in under 30 minutes on the Eventmodelers platform, with a non-technical stakeholder (his wife) following along just from the drawn screens. - **Why it's worth reading:** A ground-level counterweight to the heavy factory piece — it shows the "requirements are 80%, harness is 20%" claim at the smallest possible scale and what "pre-decided architecture" buys in speed.
