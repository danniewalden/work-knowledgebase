**2. Nick Tune — "Graphs + Memory + Skills + Agents: what you build underneath an agent" (LinkedIn, Jun 12)**
*Path: `raw/articles/nick-tune-graphs-memory-skills-agents.md` — filed, not yet ingested.*
**TLDR:** Reshares a context-engineering framework (from a PayFit AI-day talk): before "reason, act, learn" means anything, an agent needs four layers — **Graph** (the world model: what exists and how it connects), **Memory** (continuity: what was true and what changed), **Skills** (encoded judgment the agent runs), and the **Agent** loop that ties them together. **Why it's worth reading:** A crisp substrate view of agent design that rhymes with your harness-engineering and event-sourced-memory threads — the "graph + memory" framing maps onto event models and append-only event logs.

**3. `[[business-capabilities]]` — concept page (NEW, flagged important)**

**TLDR:** A business capability is the stable *what* an org can do ("Booking", "Payments"), independent of the *how*. **Why it's worth reading:** Your flagged-important topic, and it directly proposes capabilities as the natural unit of **agent ownership/blast-radius** — the bridge between the substrate and the EM×agents focus.
