## Read next

1. **Prefect (Lowin) — Loops vs. Graphs / "directed agentic graphs"** — `[[prefect-loops-vs-graphs]]` · new wiki page (07-31), the primary behind the new `[[graph-engineering]]` concept
   - **TLDR:** Jeremiah Lowin (Prefect CEO) names the layer *above* the loop: **a loop is one agent's micro behaviour; a directed agentic graph is macro orchestration across many agents.** He rebrands the old DAG (directed *acyclic* graph) as the directed *agentic* graph — nodes are agent invocations (each with its own model/tools/skills/access), edges hand control back to you. A single-node graph *is* just loop engineering; splitting into nodes buys reproducibility, auditability, and — his strongest argument — **node-level capability scoping** ("don't hand your agent a bazooka": the diligence node literally cannot issue the refund; only past a programmatic gate does the next node get a single-use, one-customer refund tool).
   - **Why worth reading:** This is the macro-orchestration counterpart to your loop-engineering thread and the cleanest control-vs-autonomy / governance framing in the KB — it turns "software factory = an org chart made of loops" into an actual control-flow diagram, and the refund example rhymes directly with your guardian-agent / DCB-guard worked models.

2. **Khononov — the AI "(s)loop" as the new Holy Grail (the lone skeptic)** — `[[khononov-microservices-hype-to-ai-sloop]]` · new wiki page (07-31)
   - **TLDR:** A short, deliberately provocative Khononov post — the KB's **only contrarian voice** on loop engineering. The argument is a historical rhyme, not a refutation: the AI "(s)loop" (loop + slop) is this decade's microservices hype — "a crowd chasing something most of them can't quite define" — and just as microservices ended in distributed monoliths, modular-monolith walkbacks, and monorepos, the loop wave will need its own hangover cure. Consistent with his standing thesis that **boundaries/modularity are what actually pay off**, so loops only deliver on a sound modular substrate.
   - **Why worth reading:** Your loop-engineering thread is otherwise built entirely from advocates (Osmani, swyx, Willison, Anthropic). This is the one page that lets you pressure-test the froth — five minutes, high signal-to-noise as a counterweight before you lean further into the loop/factory narrative.
