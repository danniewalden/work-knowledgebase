# Event Storming

A collaborative workshop technique (created by [[alberto-brandolini]]) for exploring a
business domain by mapping its **domain events** on a wall with sticky notes, bringing
developers and domain experts together to build shared understanding.

## Relationship to Event Modeling

[[event-modeling]] **evolved from** event storming. [[adam-dymitruk]] kept the workshop
format and collaborative sticky-note approach but extended it from a
requirements/scoping exercise into a **buildable blueprint** — adding commands, views,
explicit Given-When-Then specifications, and contracts between workflow steps. Where event
storming surfaces the events, event modeling turns them into something a team can implement
and cost.
