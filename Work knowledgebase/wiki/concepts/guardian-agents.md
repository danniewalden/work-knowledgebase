# Guardian Agents

**Agents that monitor other agents** — the technical enforcement layer of
[[agent-governance]], emerging as a category in its own right as autonomy increases. They
check, in real time, for compliance violations, safety failures, hallucinations, and scope
drift, verifying that an agent's actions stay within approved boundaries *before* those
actions reach customers or production.

[[gartner]] projects guardian agents will capture **10–15% of the agentic AI market by
2030**, making governance one of the fastest-growing segments. [[salesforce-agentforce]]'s
"trust layer" (data privacy, bias mitigation, hallucination prevention, confidence-threshold
escalation to humans) is a concrete instance. Conceptually a specialization of the
**parallelization/sectioning guardrail** pattern in [[agentic-workflow-patterns]], scaled to
oversee whole agents.
