# Kevin Hoffman

Director of Product Management at [[akka]] (Lightbend); author of *Applied Event Sourcing
with Akka*, *Real-World Event Sourcing*, and *Programming WebAssembly with Rust*. By his
own description he "eats, sleeps, and breathes event sourcing and distributed systems."

## Contribution

Author of the two Akka pieces arguing that [[event-sourcing]] is the foundation for
[[agentic-ai]] and that agentic systems are inherently distributed systems:

- [[akka-event-sourcing-backbone-agentic-ai]] — event sourcing gives agents perfect recall,
  auditability, and replay-based versioning.
- [[akka-agentic-systems-are-distributed-systems]] — distributed memory, streaming I/O,
  orchestration, semantic search, and regional resilience.
