# OpenAI

AI lab; maker of the GPT models and the **Codex** coding agent (incl. Codex CLI). In the KB,
OpenAI is the source of the flagship [[harness-engineering]] case study
([[openai-harness-engineering-codex]], by Ryan Lopopolo, 2026-02-11): a small team building and
shipping a ~1M-line internal product with **zero manually-written code**, all generated by Codex.

Key contributions to the harness discourse: AGENTS.md as a **table of contents** (not an
encyclopedia), **[[agent-legibility]]** as the design goal, enforcing **invariants via custom
linters and structural tests**, lint messages that inject remediation instructions
([[feedforward-and-feedback-controls]]), and "garbage-collection" agents that continuously fix
drift. Stance: "humans steer, agents execute." Sits alongside [[anthropic]] and [[langchain]] as
a primary voice on building agents.
