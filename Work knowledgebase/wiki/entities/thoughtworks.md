# Thoughtworks

Global software consultancy known for thought leadership in software engineering practices
(continuous delivery, microservices, the Technology Radar) and home to [[martin-fowler]]'s widely
read site. In the KB, Thoughtworks is the affiliation of [[birgitta-bockeler]], whose
[[harness-engineering]] mental model ([[fowler-bockeler-harness-engineering]]) draws on the firm's
delivery experience — e.g. teams "tackling architecture drift with both computational and
inferential sensors" and the cybernetics framing raised in a Technology Radar discussion.
