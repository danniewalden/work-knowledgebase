# Alberto Brandolini

Creator of **[[event-storming]]**, the collaborative, sticky-note workshop for exploring a
business domain through its **domain events**.

## Relevance to this KB

[[event-modeling]] evolved from Brandolini's [[event-storming]] — [[adam-dymitruk]] kept
the workshop format (collaboration, sticky notes on a wall) and extended it into a
buildable blueprint with commands, views, and contracts between steps.
