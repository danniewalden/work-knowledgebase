# Mitchell Hashimoto

Software engineer; founder of HashiCorp and creator of the Ghostty terminal. Credited with **naming
"harness engineering"** in his February 2026 essay [[hashimoto-my-ai-adoption-journey]] — now a
primary source in the KB (previously cited only secondhand via
[[firecrawl-what-is-an-agent-harness]]).

His framing of [[harness-engineering]] is the one the KB adopts: **anytime an agent makes a mistake,
engineer a solution so it never makes that mistake again.** Two forms: (1) **better implicit prompting**
via AGENTS.md (each line derived from an observed bad behavior); (2) **actual programmed tools**
(screenshot scripts, filtered test runners). His broader six-step adoption arc also anchors the
individual-developer end of [[unattended-coding-agents]] (background / "end-of-day" / "always have an
agent running" patterns) and stresses human control of interruptions and knowing *when not* to use an
agent.
