# Gartner

Technology research and advisory firm whose forecasts are the most-cited industry yardsticks
across the KB's agentic-AI sources. The headline 40%-by-2026 figure and the "agentwashing"
coinage are now primary-sourced from a Gartner press release
([[gartner-40-percent-enterprise-apps-task-specific-agents-2026]]); other figures still reach
the KB secondhand via [[svitla-agentic-ai-market-trends-2026]].

## Attributed forecasts and framings

- **40% of enterprise applications** will embed task-specific AI agents by end of 2026, up
  from <5% in 2025.
- **>40% of agentic AI projects** may be canceled by 2027 (cost, unclear value, weak
  governance).
- Coined **[[agentwashing]]** — the assistant-as-agent mislabeling — and the
  assistant-vs-agent distinction.
- **[[guardian-agents]]** projected at 10–15% of the agentic market by 2030; >2,000 "death
  by AI" claims forecast by end of 2026.
- By 2028: AI agents handle ~20% of digital-storefront interactions ([[agentic-commerce]]);
  ~15% of day-to-day work decisions made autonomously ([[autonomy-ladder]]); ~75% of
  developers using AI coding agents ([[agentic-coding]]).

## Caveat

These figures reach the KB **secondhand** through a vendor blog; treat as reported, not
independently verified. Worth pulling a primary Gartner release into `raw/` if precision matters.
