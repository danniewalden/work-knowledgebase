# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator; originator of the two note-taking/knowledge ideas this
knowledge base is built on.

## What he proposes

- **[[llm-wiki]]** — a pattern for personal knowledge bases where an LLM compiles
  immutable raw sources into a persistent, interlinked markdown wiki that compounds
  over time. Published as an "idea file" (see [[karpathy-llm-wiki]]).
- **[[append-and-review-note]]** — a single plain-text note where you append thoughts
  to the top, let old ones sink "under gravity," and periodically review and rescue
  what still matters.

## Notes

The two ideas complement each other: the append-and-review note is a fast personal
capture inbox; the LLM Wiki is the structured, compounding store. A thought captured in
the inbox can graduate into a curated source that gets ingested into the wiki.
