# Source: Deloitte — AI Agents Scaling Faster Than Their Guardrails

Short article by Andy Bayiates, [[deloitte]] Insights, 2026-04-24, reporting findings from
Deloitte's **2026 State of AI in the Enterprise** survey. Raw capture:
`raw/articles/deloitte-ai-agents-scaling-faster-than-guardrails.md`.

## Summary

The governance angle on the agentic boom: adoption is outpacing the controls needed to run
agents safely. Of 3,235 IT and business leaders across 24 countries, **only 21% report a
mature governance model for agentic AI** — meaning ~80% lack one even as usage scales.

## Key points

- **Adoption trajectory:** by 2027, 74% of respondents expect to use AI agents at least
  "moderately"; 23% "extensively"; 5% as a core component of operations.
- **The governance gap:** ~80% lack mature governance capabilities — clear decision
  boundaries (autonomous vs human-approval), real-time monitoring/anomaly flagging, and
  audit trails of the full chain of agent actions.
- **Risks of ungoverned agents:** unseen mistakes, agents working at cross purposes, leaking
  sensitive information, offending customers, inviting cyberattacks — risks that *compound*
  when pilots scale to production.
- **What winners do:** start with lower-risk use cases, build governance capability, scale
  deliberately, and stand up **cross-functional governance** (IT, legal, compliance, business).
- **Thesis:** retrofitting oversight later is slower and costlier than building guardrails
  upfront; "putting safety first" beats "moving first."
