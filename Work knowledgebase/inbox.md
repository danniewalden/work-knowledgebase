# Inbox — append & review

Karpathy's [append-and-review note](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/the-append-and-review-note/),
as a single capture surface. One note, no folders, no tags. `Ctrl+F` is the search.

**How it works**
- **Append:** any thought, todo, link, or quote → add it to the **top** of the list below.
- **Gravity:** as new things land on top, old ones sink toward the bottom.
- **Review:** now and then, scroll down and skim. Rescue anything still worth attention by
  copy-pasting it back to the top; merge related notes. Things that don't earn attention
  keep sinking. Rarely delete.
- **Promote:** when a note becomes a real source worth keeping, move it into `raw/` and ask
  me to ingest it into the wiki.

Optional tags for skimming later: `watch:`, `listen:`, `read:`.
