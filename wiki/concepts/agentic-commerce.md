# Agentic Commerce

The emerging pattern where **AI agents make purchasing decisions on behalf of consumers and
businesses** — evaluating options, comparing prices, and completing transactions
([[svitla-agentic-ai-market-trends-2026]]).

[[gartner]] predicts that by 2028 AI agents will handle ~20% of interactions at digital
storefronts designed for humans; reportedly 70% of consumers already use agents for travel
bookings and 59% for electronics (mainly price comparison and personalization).

The strategic implication: the "customer" changes. When an agent is the buyer, the storefront
must satisfy an agent's criteria rather than persuade a human — so **product data, pricing
transparency, and API accessibility** start to matter more than visual design and emotional
marketing. Still early, but consequential for e-commerce architecture and SEO. Depends on the
agent-to-agent infrastructure of [[agent2agent-protocol]] for buyer/seller agent interaction.
