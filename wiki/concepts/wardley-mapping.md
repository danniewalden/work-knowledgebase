# Wardley Mapping

**Concept stub** (added 2026-06-14 to support the Event Modeling ↔ Wardley link Dannie asked for). A
strategy technique from **Simon Wardley**: plot the components a business needs to serve a user need on
a map with two axes — **value chain / visibility to the user** (vertical) and **evolution**
(horizontal: Genesis → Custom-Built → Product/Rental → Commodity/Utility). The map exposes *where* each
component sits and *how it is evolving*, informing strategic plays (build vs buy, where to invest, what
is differentiating vs. utility).

## Relation to the focus

Pairs with [[business-capabilities]] and [[event-modeling]]: capabilities (Homann,
[[homann-business-capabilities]]) name the stable "what"; a Wardley map shows how each capability is
**evolving** and therefore how to treat it strategically; [[event-modeling]] then renders the chosen
direction as a concrete, deterministic system design. **Chris Daniel**'s series
([[daniel-event-modeling-wardley-mapping]]) is the documented pairing — Wardley says *where to go and
why*, Event Modeling says *what to build*. Adam Dymitruk has also referenced the connection.
