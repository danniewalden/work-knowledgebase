# The Event Modeling and Event Sourcing Podcast

A weekly podcast co-hosted by **[[adam-dymitruk]]** (creator of [[event-modeling]], [[adaptech-group]])
and **[[martin-dilger]]** ([[nebulit]], author of *Understanding Eventsourcing*, building
[[eventmodelers-ai]]). Tagline: "Your weekly podcast about Event Modeling and Event Sourcing." It runs
on [podcast.eventmodeling.org](https://podcast.eventmodeling.org/), as video on the YouTube channel
[@eventdrivenpodcast](https://www.youtube.com/@eventdrivenpodcast)
([playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLYNJUeYuZDAP0luNOf5MZAbnzPWRCnZyp)), and on
[Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/5yIPErSiVqs2fkMia3WbwZ).

- **First episode:** 2024-11-04 ("Destroying the Aggregate"). **Season Two** opens at Ep 23
  (2025-11-08, "AI Takes Your Job"). 46 episodes as of the 2026-06-17 capture (latest Ep 46,
  2026-04-27).
- **Format:** unscripted conversation — workshop debriefs, design arguments, tooling updates, and
  personal/maker tangents. Episodes typically run ~30–90 min.
- **Why it's here:** it is the **running practitioner commentary** behind the two people who anchor this
  KB's [[event-modeling]] / [[event-modeled-agent-design]] thread — the place their positions get
  argued out weekly before showing up in posts, talks, and books.

## What it covers (see the source page for the through-lines)

Destroying/shrinking the aggregate and [[dynamic-consistency-boundaries|DCB]]; simplicity; sagas → to-do
lists; [[vertical-slice-architecture|slices]] as the unit of work; [[given-when-then]] specs;
[[event-versioning-and-upcasting|schema migration / upcasting]]; [[event-sourcing]] as old-and-
foundational; and — dominating Season Two — AI / [[agentic-coding]], the [[ralph-loop]],
[[vibe-modeling]], and "Event Modeling 2.0." Business model: fixed-price *pay-per-slice* consulting and
workshops; Ep 23 announces a new event-modeling company and a push to **standardize** the tooling
([[eventmodelers-ai]]).
