# Solace

Event-broker and event-mesh vendor; markets "Solace Agent Mesh," an agentic-AI platform built on
event-driven integration. Roger Sabourin leads its analyst-relations program.

In this KB, Solace is the publisher of [[solace-multi-agent-systems-real-time-context-eda]]
(Mar 2026), which synthesizes [[gartner]] and [[idc]] research to argue that **multi-agent systems
require [[event-driven-architecture]] and real-time context**. Like [[confluent]] and [[atlan]], a
vendor with a stake in the EDA conclusion — but the piece is unusually analyst-citation-heavy, so
the forecasts and the "inner/outer agent architecture" and "EDA for MAGS" claims trace to Gartner/IDC
rather than to Solace itself.
