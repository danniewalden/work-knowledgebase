# Pramod Sadalage

Distinguished Engineer at [[thoughtworks]], leading its Data Engineering and Architecture service for
North America. In the early 2000s he developed the techniques for **evolutionary database design** via
version-controlled schema migrations — the lineage that makes his "schema is law, data contracts as
code" position in 2026 a continuation rather than a reversal. Author on five books including
*Refactoring Databases* and *Software Architecture: The Hard Parts*.

[[autonomy-ladder]] and [[prompt-injection]].
