# Drew Breunig

Independent writer at **dbreunig.com**. Three source pages in this KB, and — unusually for this thread —
**he has no product in the space**, which is his main virtue as a source. He entered the KB via
[[simon-willison]]'s 2026-08-23 quotation of him.

## What he contributes

- **The eight-layer "situated agent" definition of a harness**
  ([[breunig-harnesses-are-situated-agents]]) — the KB's most structural answer to "what *is* a harness",
  and the frame [[agent-harness]] now leans on.
- **The end-of-the-free-lunch economics of harness work**
  ([[breunig-fable-and-the-end-of-the-free-lunch]]) — why harness effort stops being repaid
  automatically by the next model. Reads directly against
  [[harness-absorption|the train → absorb → shed account]].
- **The attribution argument about agent incidents**
  ([[breunig-who-taught-the-models-to-do-that]]) — that behaviours behind agent incidents were *trained*,
  not emergent from the harness, which relocates responsibility. Note the figures in that piece are
  **Anthropic's own** (Opus 4.1 reward hacking "52% → 18%", "65% less likely… if you simply asked it not
  to"): **VENDOR SELF-REPORT** — own model, own system card, own internal benchmark, no replication, and
  the delta sits inside a programme of other post-training changes. The marker travels with the numbers;
  the *argument* is his.

**Standing note.** Independent of the vendors, but everything here is **essayistic argument, not
measurement** — he relays others' numbers and reasons about them. Named gap: his companion
**"Overfitting the Harness" (2026-05-10)** is cited but **uncaptured**.
