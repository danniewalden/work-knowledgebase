# Andrew Miracle

**Head of Product & Research at Tecmie**, and author of the KB's **most fully specified
single-practitioner rig** ([[miracle-my-loop-engineering-workflow]]) — published as configuration at
`github.com/koolamusic/claudefiles`, so it can be read rather than inferred.

## What he contributes

- **The triangle of loops** — his own decomposition of the loop stack.
- **Goal-backward verification** — deriving the check from the goal rather than from the change.
- **The Amdahl-at-the-join argument** — that the serial join is what caps parallel agent work, which is
  the sharpest mechanism the KB holds for why fan-out saturates.
- **The trust ledger** — a running record of what a given agent has earned autonomy for
  ([[autonomy-ladder]]).

**Standing note, and it is the whole caveat: everything here is configuration, and nothing is
measured.** No baseline, no comparison, no failure accounting. Its value is that the rig is *legible* —
a specimen to read against other people's descriptions, not evidence that it works.
