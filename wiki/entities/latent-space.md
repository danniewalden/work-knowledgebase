# Latent.Space

A **paywalled Substack** (podcast + newsletter, including the AINews daily roundup), founded by
[[swyx]]. It is now **one of the KB's main conduits for AI-engineering practitioner interviews and
conference reporting**, and the venue [[laurie-voss]] cites for AIEWF coverage.

**This is a venue page, not an author page** — recorded as such deliberately. Its interviewer,
**Richard MacManus**, interviews rather than argues, so the three MacManus captures in the KB are
attributed here rather than to a person page. Guest essays run here too
([[mcateer-evolution-of-the-agent-harness|Dan McAteer]]).

## Standing note — treat product claims as vendor claims

The format is **interviews with founders about their own products**, so anything an interviewee says
about their own tool is a **VENDOR SELF-REPORT reaching the KB at second hand**. Two examples the KB has
already refused:

- The AI SDK factory's *"authors 25–35% of merged PRs, closes 70–80% of issues"* — **VENDOR
  SELF-REPORT**, Vercel on Vercel, relayed, **four weeks after implementation**, no methodology and no
  regression or reopen data.
- *"It's totally shifted in the last six months"* and *"never seen that in my entire decade-plus"* —
  **IMPRESSION NOT MEASUREMENT**; never render either as a figure or a rate.

Also relevant: it is **paywalled**, so captures may not be independently checkable by a later reader.
