# Ulrich Homann

Microsoft architect (Distinguished Architect / CTO roles over his career) best known in this KB as
author of **"A Business-Oriented Foundation for Service Orientation"** (Feb 2006,
[[homann-business-capabilities]]) — the seminal articulation of **business-capability mapping**:
modeling a business as a network of capability "black boxes" (what, not how) with semantic connectors,
organized as a nested taxonomy. The paper is a foundational reference for business architecture (cited
by the BIZBOK Guide).

Anchors the [[business-capabilities]] concept in the KB.
