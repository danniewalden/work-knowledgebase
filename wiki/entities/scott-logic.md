# Scott Logic

UK software consultancy. **[[colin-eberhardt]] is its CTO.** Publishes at blog.scottlogic.com under
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

It is in the KB for exactly one thing: it is the publisher of
[[eberhardt-putting-spec-kit-through-its-paces]] (2025-11-26), **the most instrumented
[[spec-driven-development|SDD]] trial the KB holds**. Before that capture was ingested the wiki
referred to it only as an unnamed "Scott Logic SpecKit trial," reached second-hand.

**Marker:** a consultancy with a **commercial interest in development services** — not a neutral
research body. That is the relevant caveat when its material is cited as evidence about how software
should be built. (The trial itself is on Eberhardt's own hobby project, not a client engagement.)

**Nothing else about the company is evidenced by the KB's captures**, and nothing further is asserted
here.
