# Marmelab

French development agency. **[[francois-zaninotto]] is its founder and CEO.** Maintainers of
**react-admin** and **Atomic CRM** — the latter being the codebase used in the Kiro example that
[[birgitta-bockeler]] examines.

It is in the KB as the publisher of
[[zaninotto-spec-driven-development-waterfall-strikes-back]] (2025-11-12) and its **seven failure
modes** of [[spec-driven-development]] — the fullest such list the KB holds. Before that capture was
ingested the wiki cited it only as "**Marmelab**, independently," relayed in one sentence through
[[ng-spec-driven-development-is-waterfall-in-markdown|Ng]].

**Marker:** an agency with a **commercial interest in development services** — not a neutral research
body. Note in particular that the piece's counter-example (a 3D sculpting tool built in *"about 10
hours"* with no spec) is **its own founder's project on its own blog**: **NOT INDEPENDENT · self-report**.

**Nothing else about the company is evidenced by the KB's captures**, and nothing further is asserted
here.
