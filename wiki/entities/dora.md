# DORA (DevOps Research and Assessment)

A long-running software-delivery research program, now **run by Google Cloud**, best known for the
**DORA delivery metrics** (deploy frequency, lead time, change-fail rate, time-to-restore) and the annual
*Accelerate State of DevOps* / *State of AI-Assisted Software Development* reports. Team lead: **Nathen
Harvey**. All dora.dev content is licensed **CC BY 4.0**. DORA research is the empirical lineage
[[team-topologies]] was built on (per [[matthew-skelton]]).

## Where it appears in this KB

- **[[dora-roi-ai-assisted-software-development-2026|ROI of AI-Assisted Software Development report
  (2026.01)]]** — the primary that promoted this page from a future-marker. Central claims: AI is an
  **amplifier** of the organizational system; the **J-Curve** of value realisation (a productivity dip =
  learning curve + verification tax + downstream adaptation); an ROI chain (AI → seven capabilities →
  DORA metrics → DevEx/UX → cost/revenue); the **instability tax**; and an agentic-era reframing (ROI =
  latent human creativity unlocked, *not* headcount replaced). Captured via a secondary — the primary is
  a gated Google Cloud download.
- **[[skelton-team-topologies-foundation-ai-roi]]** — Skelton reads the 2026 DORA ROI report as the case
  that **value-flow-aligned team boundaries** are the precondition for AI ROI.
- **[[agent-governance]]** — DORA's finding that inference cost has fallen ~280× relocates the real cost
  of AI adoption **from compute to governance** (managing the verification tax).

## Prior DORA research referenced (not yet separately captured)

- **2025 State of AI-Assisted Software Development** — introduced the **DORA AI Capabilities Model** and
  seven team archetypes; ~5,000 professionals surveyed + 100+ hours of interviews; the "AI amplifies /
  AI will not fix broken engineering systems" finding; ~30% of developers trust AI output "a little" or
  "not at all." A candidate next capture if the capabilities-model detail is needed.
