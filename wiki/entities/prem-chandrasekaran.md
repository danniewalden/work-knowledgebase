# Prem Chandrasekaran

Premanand ("Prem") Chandrasekaran is a Market Tech Director at [[thoughtworks]], leading technology
strategy and delivery across a client portfolio while staying hands-on with production systems. His
stated current focus is **how AI can augment the software development lifecycle** — architecture,
design, coding, testing, operations — "so that developers can move faster without sacrificing clarity,
safety, or maintainability," which puts him adjacent to this KB's [[agentic-coding]] thread as well as
its capability thread.
