# Nebulit GmbH

The company founded by **[[martin-dilger]]**, through which he does Event Modeling / event-sourcing
consulting and training across the DACH region and is building **[[eventmodelers-ai]]**. Named in his
Craft Conference speaker bio ([[dilger-craft-conf-idea-to-event-model-to-code]]), which credits him
with 20+ years in software architecture, helping teams "align business and engineering, close
requirements gaps, and deliver event-driven systems," and authoring *"Understanding Eventsourcing,"*
the first German-language book on Event Modeling.

Functionally the commercial vehicle behind the Dilger material in this KB — the consultancy/vendor
context for his [[event-modeled-agent-design]] evangelism (cf. [[adaptech-group]] for
[[adam-dymitruk]]).
