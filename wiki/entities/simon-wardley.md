# Simon Wardley

Researcher and strategist; creator of **[[wardley-mapping]]** and author of *Wardley Maps:
Topographical intelligence in business* (serialized on Medium under Creative Commons; CC BY-SA).
Associated with the Leading Edge Forum (LEF/DXC).

## Contribution

- Devised the **Wardley map**: components needed to serve a user need, plotted on **value chain /
  visibility** (vertical) × **evolution** (horizontal: Genesis → Custom Built → Product → Commodity),
  to reason about strategy ([[wardley-maps-value-chains-and-evolution]]).
- The core method: **start from the user need, work backwards** down the value chain, then place each
  component by its **evolution stage** — driving the build-vs-buy / differentiate-vs-optimize decisions.
- Broader framework (not captured here): doctrine, climatic patterns, gameplay, and the
  pioneers/settlers/town-planners organizational model.
