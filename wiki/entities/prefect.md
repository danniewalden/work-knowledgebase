# Prefect

Workflow-orchestration company led by [[jeremiah-lowin]] (CEO). Historically a
Python data-workflow orchestrator; in the agentic era Prefect (and its FastMCP
line) is the vehicle through which Lowin advances the **directed agentic graph**
framing — macro orchestration *across* many agents, as distinct from the loop that
governs one agent's internal behaviour (see [[graph-engineering]], [[loop-engineering]]).

The framing is laid out in the Prefect/FastMCP podcast episode captured at
[[prefect-loops-vs-graphs]], where Lowin contrasts loops (LangGraph / Pydantic-AI,
one agent's behaviour) with directed agentic graphs (nodes = agent invocations with
per-node tools/model/access; edges hand control back), grounded in a worked
refund-agent example.
