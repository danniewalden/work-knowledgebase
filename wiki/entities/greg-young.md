# Greg Young

Software architect associated with **[[cqrs]]** and **[[event-sourcing]]**, and with the
long-running process / "process manager" specifications in CQRS/ES systems.

## Relevance to this KB

[[adam-dymitruk]] cites Young's long-running process specifications as one of the
foundations he built **[[event-modeling]]** on (the other being Alberto Brandolini's
[[event-storming]]). The "Event Store" category of databases — an ordered, guaranteed-replay
log — comes out of this CQRS/ES lineage.
