# Markus Borg

Software-engineering researcher working at the intersection of **CodeScene** and academia (with Lund
University, where co-author Emma Söderberg is based). Lead author of the empirical studies validating
CodeScene's **CodeHealth (CH)** metric — first against human maintainability judgments and defect/effort
data (Tornhill & Borg 2022; Borg et al. 2024), and now against **AI-friendliness**.

## Position in the KB

The academic lead behind [[borg-tornhill-code-for-machines-not-just-humans]] (FORGE 2026), the
peer-reviewed grounding for the code-health × [[agentic-coding]] thread that [[adam-tornhill]] popularizes
in talks and posts. Borg supplies the rigor — controlled experiments, a test-pass oracle, predictor
comparisons (CH vs. perplexity vs. SLOC) — distinguishing the research result (**15–30% refactoring-risk
reduction in Healthy code**) from CodeScene's marketing figures.
