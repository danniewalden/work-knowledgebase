# Deloitte

Global professional-services firm; via **Deloitte Insights** it publishes enterprise
technology research. In the KB, Deloitte supplies the independent enterprise-survey evidence
on agentic AI governance.

## Relevance to this KB

- Publisher of the **2026 State of AI in the Enterprise** survey (3,235 IT/business leaders,
  24 countries), the basis for [[deloitte-ai-agents-scaling-faster-than-guardrails]].
- Key finding the KB leans on: **only 21% of organizations have mature agentic-AI
  governance**, framing the central [[agent-governance]] gap.
- Provides a counterweight to vendor-authored sources ([[akka]], [[langchain]],
  [[svitla-systems]]) — useful for the KB's evenhandedness.
