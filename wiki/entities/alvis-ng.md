# Alvis Ng

A **technical lead** who writes on Medium about software, product, faith, psychology and AI (~1.8K
followers; self-described as writing about "what's not mentioned in meetings"). Not a tracked voice in the
KB's research watch — he enters the wiki through a single Dannie-directed ingest.

## Why he's here

[[ng-spec-driven-development-is-waterfall-in-markdown]] (2026-03-16) was the KB's **first captured**
outside-in critique of [[spec-driven-development]] — *not the first written*. Corrected 2026-08-31: the
primaries hunt captured three earlier originals he is downstream of (Böckeler 2025-10-15, Zaninotto
2025-11-12, Eberhardt 2025-11-26), so he is a synthesiser of an Oct–Nov 2025 critique wave rather than its
origin. That does not diminish him — the provenance argument below is his own — but his value to the KB
is **synthesis, not evidence**. His contribution is twofold:

1. **He assembles the external empirical record** the [[spec-driven-development]] page had been asking
   for — Scott Logic's ~10x-slower SpecKit trial, the Fowler/Böckeler Kiro analysis, Augment Engineer's
   1,300-lines-of-Markdown-to-render-a-date (the real primary for that figure is
   [[zaninotto-spec-driven-development-waterfall-strikes-back|Zaninotto]], not Ng — cite it there),
   Marmelab's "The Waterfall Strikes Back", and
   [[gojko-adzic]] calling SDD "the revenge of waterfall or BDD taken to a new level." None of it is his
   own data — **and the Adzic line is a misreading he introduced**: it is the title of Adzic's post, posed
   as a question and answered "it does not, really." See [[gojko-adzic]].
2. **He names the interface problem**: a spec flattens a cross-functional set of mental models "into a
   single voice: yours," and becomes "a contract between you and the LLM that nobody else signed." His
   replacement is [[decision-trace|structure harvested from recorded conversations]] rather than authored
   up front.

## How to read him

He is a practitioner essayist, not a researcher: his own SDD trial was a **solo personal project** and the
team-level failure is reasoned rather than observed, every empirical claim is secondhand, and his proposed
workflow carries no evaluation of its own. Useful for the argument and as a **pointer list to primaries
worth capturing**; not a source to cite for numbers without going to the originals.

He is also not arguing against modelling as such — his position that structure must come from the room is
compatible with, and arguably an argument *for*, the shared-artifact claim in [[event-modeled-agent-design]].
