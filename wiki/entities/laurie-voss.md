# Laurie Voss

Author of the KB's **disambiguation primary** for [[loop-engineering]] —
[[voss-what-the-hell-is-a-loop-anyway]], published on O'Reilly Radar / LinkedIn, **2026-07-29**.

## What he contributes

- **A four-loop map** — execution / task / product / system — which is the KB's cleanest answer to the
  question the loop literature usually skips: *which* loop is being discussed.
- **The oversight loop**, which he names as a fifth thing sitting across the others.
- **The feedback criterion**, the most quotable line in the KB on the subject: *"a loop without feedback
  is just a `for` statement."*
- The observation that **autonomy is a dial per loop**, not a single global setting.

**Standing notes.** The piece **closes with a vendor plug for Arize AX** (his sponsor/employer context),
and **every figure in it is second-hand** — including [[anthropic]]'s "65% of its product team's code"
via Claude Tag and Warp's "ratcheting auto-merge from 20% toward 60%", both **VENDOR SELF-REPORT,
relayed**, and the Warp figure is a stated *target path*, not an achieved result. Cite him for the
taxonomy, not for numbers.
