# Dan McAteer

Agentic engineer and writer of **Attention Heads** ("essays on AI, human attention, philosophy, and
contemplative practice"); guest author at **[[latent-space|Latent.Space]]**.

The KB holds **one** capture: [[mcateer-evolution-of-the-agent-harness]] (2026-08-22). It contributes
two things now carried on concept pages:

- the **two-curves / train → absorb → shed → repeat** account of model–harness co-evolution — the model
  and harness curves *crossed*, they did not leap, so progress is measured by **how much harness you can
  delete** ([[harness-absorption]]);
- the coinage of the **[[attention-interface]]** — the harness inverting into an interface for scarce
  *human* attention.

**Marker, and it is the reason this page exists: he is a commentator relaying other people's figures,
not a primary researcher.** Every number in his piece is secondhand, and the **Harness-Bench** figure he
popularised (52.4 → 76.2 across 106 tasks with zero model change) has **no primary capture in this KB** —
the benchmark is still author-less and venue-less. Cite him for the *framing*, never as the source of the
number.

Nothing further about him is evidenced by the capture; no employer, role or history is asserted here.
