# Akka (Lightbend)

Company and platform (Lightbend, Inc. dba Akka) for building distributed, event-driven
systems. In 2025 it positioned itself as a foundation for **[[agentic-ai]]** at enterprise
scale, with products spanning agents, orchestration, memory, and streaming.

## Notes

- Core thesis in its content (via [[kevin-hoffman]]): [[event-sourcing]] is the backbone of
  agentic systems, and agentic systems are distributed systems — so an agentic platform must
  be a distributed-systems platform.
- Markets **Akka Automated Operations** for multi-region resilience/failover.
- Cited customer: Manulife ("selects Akka to operationalize agentic AI").
