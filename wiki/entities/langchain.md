# LangChain

Company behind the open-source **LangChain** and **LangGraph** agent frameworks and the
**LangSmith** observability/evaluation platform. In the KB it is both a tooling vendor and
the author of an annual practitioner survey.

## Relevance to this KB

- Publisher of the **[[langchain-state-of-agent-engineering-2026]]** survey (1,340
  respondents), the KB's main empirical read on production adoption, use cases, and blockers.
- Champions **[[agent-engineering]]** as a discipline and **[[agent-observability-and-evals]]**
  as table-stakes practice (LangSmith is its commercial answer).
- LangChain/LangGraph are the most-cited stack for building **custom agents** in that survey.
- Authored **[[langchain-anatomy-of-an-agent-harness]]** (Vivek Trivedy), the KB's
  first-principles definition of the **[[agent-harness]]** ("Agent = Model + Harness") and its
  core primitives; builds the **`deepagents`** harness library. Harrison Chase's recurring
  argument: harnesses *grow* as models improve, not shrink.
- A vendor with skin in the game (sells observability/eval tooling), so its emphasis on
  observability should be read with that interest in mind.
