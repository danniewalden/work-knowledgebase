# ChronosHub

The organization behind this knowledge base (Dannie's employer). Appears in the KB as a practitioner
org that has adopted **[[event-modeling|Event Modeling]] + [[event-sourcing|Event Sourcing]]** as its
engineering strategy, with an explicit orientation toward the **agentic future**.

## Contribution / position

- Via [[christian-johansen]]'s [[johansen-is-it-safe-to-jump-em-conf-2026|"Is it safe to jump?"]]
  (2026-07-01): ChronosHub is "in a rare position" — it already has **leadership buy-in** for an
  engineering strategy built on Event Modeling and Event Sourcing, rather than having to sell the method
  bottom-up.
- Frames its "**architecture and strategy for the agentic future**" as a deliberate bet — the
  Event Modeling Conference 2026 "sharpened" but did not change that direction.
- Carries the open **experience-engineering × agents** question: having built the systems, the next
  problem is shaping the interaction/experience layer for both humans and AI agents
  ([[event-modeled-agent-design]]).
