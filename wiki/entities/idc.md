# IDC

International Data Corporation — IT market-research and analyst firm; cited alongside [[gartner]] as
a source of agentic-AI forecasts in the KB.

Cited claim (via [[solace-multi-agent-systems-real-time-context-eda]], "FutureScape: Worldwide Data
and Analytics 2026 Predictions"): **by 2027, 80% of agentic-AI use cases will require real-time,
contextual, and ubiquitous data access, forcing a majority of G2000 firms to shift from gatekeeper
data models to a federated approach.** Used in the KB to support the "real-time context as
prerequisite for multi-agent systems" argument ([[multi-agent-orchestration]],
[[event-driven-architecture]]). Currently secondhand (quoted by a vendor); the primary IDC report is
not in `raw/`.
