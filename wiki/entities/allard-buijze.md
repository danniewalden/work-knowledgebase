# Allard Buijze

Founder & CTO of [[axoniq]] and the original creator of **Axon Framework**, one of the most widely
used [[event-sourcing]]/[[cqrs]] frameworks on the JVM. A foundational practitioner voice in the
event-sourcing community, alongside [[greg-young]] and [[sara-pellegrini]].

## Position in the KB

Co-author (with CEO Jessica Reeves) of the AxonIQ explainability piece
([[axoniq-ai-agent-explainability-why-infrastructure-needs-to-remember]]). His framing of the
developer shift — *"stop designing systems around data, start designing them around decisions and
events"* — is the event-sourcing thesis applied to AI agents, and he positions the **event store**
(captures *why*) against the **event stream** (moves *what*). Axon Framework 5, under his direction,
makes [[dynamic-consistency-boundaries|DCB]] its flagship capability.

At the **first Event Modeling Conference** ([[dilger-first-event-modeling-conference-munich-recap]],
Munich Oct 2025) he delivered the Day-2 keynote demoing the **new AxonIQ platform turning Event Models
into working code automatically** — the most concrete "agent generates from the model" artifact the KB
has seen for [[event-modeled-agent-design]] (vendor-staged), and was a principal voice in the
conference's DCB-vs-aggregates debate.
