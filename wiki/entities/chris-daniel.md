# Krzysztof "Chris" Daniel

Mapping consultant, researcher and advisor (DXC Leading Edge Forum), where he works alongside
[[simon-wardley]]; author of an online Wardley Mapping course and an active member of the Wardley
Mapping community. Maintainer of **WardleyMaps.com**, where he hosts and **adapts/expands** Wardley's
CC-licensed book ([[wardley-maps-value-chains-and-evolution]]). Built an online mapping tool. (Goes by
both "Chris Daniel" and "Krzysztof Daniel"; X/Twitter handle `@wardleymaps`.)

## In the KB — the EM × Wardley bridge

Daniel produced the documented pairing of **[[wardley-mapping]]** with **[[event-modeling]]** — a 4-part
YouTube series (2020, [[daniel-event-modeling-wardley-mapping]]): Wardley positions
components/[[business-capabilities|capabilities]] on the value-chain × evolution plane to decide
*where to go and why*; Event Modeling then renders the chosen direction as a concrete, deterministic
system design (*what to build*). He is therefore the connective tissue in the **capabilities × strategy
× design** trio Dannie flagged. (The series transcript is still uncaptured — verbatim record remains a
to-do; his WardleyMaps.com adaptation is the captured proxy.)
