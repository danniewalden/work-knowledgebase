# Andy Wong

Practitioner writer at **awongcm.io**, running a series that climbs the
**context → harness → loop → graph** ladder. Two source pages in this KB
([[wong-loop-engineering-teaching-ai-agents-how-to-think]],
[[wong-graph-engineering-wiring-agents-into-an-organization]]).

**He is valuable less for the ladder than for one act of intellectual honesty:** he **published the
provenance of "graph engineering" against his own interest** — that it started as half a joke, went
viral *as* a joke, was contested as marketing, and was then formalised in a preprint. That is the
KB's cleanest account of how a term on this thread actually came to exist, from the person with the most
to gain from treating it as settled.

**Standing note:** his own framing in the graph piece is **prospective** — an argument about what should
work, not a report of what did. Nothing in either capture is measured.
