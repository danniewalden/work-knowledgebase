# Matthew Skelton

Co-author (with Manuel Pais) of **[[team-topologies]]** (IT Revolution, 2019) and founder of **Conflux**.
In this KB he is the author of *"Why Team Topologies Is the Essential Foundation for AI ROI"*
([[skelton-team-topologies-foundation-ai-roi]], teamtopologies.com, 2026-07-04).

## Position — value-flow boundaries as the foundation for AI ROI

Reading the **2026 DORA ROI report** ([[dora]]; now captured — [[dora-roi-ai-assisted-software-development-2026]]),
Skelton argues **AI is an amplifier**: it magnifies a
high-performing organization's strengths and a struggling one's dysfunctions alike, so ROI comes from the
underlying *organizational system* (platform quality, workflow clarity, team alignment), not the tools.
His agent-era claim: teams **structurally aligned to continuous flows of value** (handoffs eliminated)
give autonomous AI agents "clear objectives and anchoring points … to understand what to build and how
to build it" — the **value-flow-aligned team boundary is the boundary that makes an agent effective.**
He also pushes **data as a curated product**, digestible for both human engineers *and* agents (clean,
domain-aligned APIs), and reframes the "verification tax" of AI-amplified output as work for **enabling
/ platform teams** to automate.

## In the KB

Skelton anchors the [[team-topologies]] concept (previously ingested only via the
[[coupling-research-note]]) with a **named author** and its agent-era extension. His organization-design
argument is the counterpart to [[john-devadoss|deVadoss's]] architecture-design argument
([[devadoss-cead-capability-aligned-agent-design]]): both put the durable value/capability boundary
before the tooling, and both are agent-era readings of [[conways-law]]. Connects to
[[business-capabilities]] (a team stewarding its own contracted capability/data) and
[[agent-legibility]] (data as a product agents can read).
