# Salesforce Agentforce

Salesforce's agentic-AI platform for deploying task-specific agents (notably in customer
service and operations). In the KB it is the most-cited commercial deployment example,
standing in for "agentic AI delivering measurable enterprise results."

## Reported metrics (via Svitla)

- **>18,000 customers** across 121 countries; **330% YoY ARR** growth by Q3 FY2026.
- **$1.4B ARR** combined with Data Cloud 360; 9,500+ paid deals.
- Handled **>380,000 customer support interactions**, resolving **84% of cases
  autonomously**, escalating only 2% to humans.
- Case studies: IRS (tax-court case openings 10 days → 30 min); a Fortune 500 report cycle
  (15 days → 35 min, $2,200 → $9 per report).
- Built a **trust layer** (data privacy, bias mitigation, hallucination prevention,
  confidence-threshold escalation) — a concrete instance of [[guardian-agents]] /
  [[agent-governance]].

## Relevance

Demonstrates [[customer-support-agents]] and [[agent-governance]] in production, and is a
named driver of the market growth in [[svitla-agentic-ai-market-trends-2026]]. All figures
are vendor-reported via a secondary source — treat as illustrative, not audited.
