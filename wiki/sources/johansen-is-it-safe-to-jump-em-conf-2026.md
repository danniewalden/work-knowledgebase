# Johansen — Is it safe to jump? (Event Modeling Conference 2026)

Source: [[christian-johansen]] ([[chronoshub]]), *"Is it safe to jump?"*, LinkedIn post,
**2026-07-01** (surfaced via a [[martin-dilger]] repost; tags `#eventmodeling #specdriven #agentic
#chronoshub`). Raw capture: `raw/notes/johansen-is-it-safe-to-jump-em-conf-2026.md`. The
long-watched **practitioner write-up of the 2nd Event Modeling Conference** (Munich, June 2026), and
notable to this KB as a public statement of **[[chronoshub|ChronosHub]]'s** — Dannie's own org's — EM +
event-sourcing + agentic strategy.

## Summary

A reflection from the Event Modeling Conference 2026 in Munich, framed around the Eisbach river in the
English Garden: during a heatwave, jumping into the (officially prohibited) standing wave feels unsafe
until you see hundreds already in the water — then "the risk suddenly felt manageable. So we jumped."
Johansen turns the metaphor on the method itself: **why are only a handful doing Event Modeling, and how
do we bring the rest of the software world along?**

## Key points

- **The community's problem is narrative, not technique.** "The community is doing something remarkable,
  but it struggles to tell a coherent story about itself." It is **fragmented** across [[event-sourcing|Event
  Sourcing]], [[cqrs|CQRS]], [[domain-driven-design|DDD]], [[event-modeling|Event Modeling]], and Domain
  Storytelling — each camp with its own vocabulary, canonical examples, and conventions.
- **Adoption is gated on the pitch, not the payoff.** Explaining EM to an enterprise "can feel like
  teaching someone to become a mechanic rather than just handing them the keys to a car." To grow, the
  community must **speak to leadership, not just engineers** — an adoption-strategy claim, distinct from
  the method's technical merits.
- **ChronosHub already has leadership buy-in.** It is "in a rare position": an engineering strategy built
  on Event Modeling + Event Sourcing with leadership backing. The conference "didn't change our direction
  but sharpened our thinking," and left "a reinforced conviction that our **architecture and strategy for
  the agentic future** are pointing in the right direction."
- **Open question — Event Modeling × *experience engineering*.** The seam Johansen leaves unresolved:
  how does EM intersect with **crafting interaction experiences that work seamlessly for both humans and
  AI agents**? "We're already building the systems. The harder, more interesting question is how to shape
  the experience layer on top of them." A new axis for the KB — the *experience* layer over
  [[event-modeled-agent-design|agent-facing systems]], beyond system design.

## Why it matters

Three things. First, it is the **first substantive practitioner reflection on the 2026 Event Modeling
Conference** the watch has been waiting on (prior runs only ever surfaced the *first* conference's recap
[[dilger-first-event-modeling-conference-munich-recap]] and the login-walled `#eventmodelingconference2026`
posts). Second, it is a rare **public strategy statement from ChronosHub** naming EM + ES + "the agentic
future" as its direction — directly relevant to Dannie. Third, it opens an **experience-engineering ×
agents** question the KB had not tracked: designing not just the system but the human-and-agent
*interaction experience* on top of it, adjacent to [[event-modeled-agent-design]] and
[[spec-driven-development]]. Caveats: a short LinkedIn reflection (not a technical artifact); vendor/
community-insider vantage; the "speak to leadership" thesis is asserted, not evidenced.
