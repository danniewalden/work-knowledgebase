# Daniel — Event Modeling and Wardley Mapping

Pointer source: **Chris Daniel**'s 4-part YouTube series (2020) pairing [[wardley-mapping]] with
[[event-modeling]]. Captured 2026-06-14 as the documented EM↔Wardley link Dannie asked for. **Stub** —
the video wasn't transcribed; thesis is second-hand. Raw:
`raw/articles/daniel-event-modeling-and-wardley-mapping.md`.

## Key points (summarized, second-hand)

- **Complementary, not competing.** [[wardley-mapping]] = *strategy* (position components/capabilities
  on value-chain × evolution; decide build/buy, differentiating vs utility). [[event-modeling]] =
  *concrete deterministic design* (commands/events/read-models timeline).
- **Sequence:** Wardley tells you *where to go and why*; Event Modeling tells you *what to build*.
- Bridges [[business-capabilities]] (the stable "what", [[homann-business-capabilities]]) to how each
  capability is *evolving* and therefore how to treat it.

## Update (2026-06-15)

The author is **[[chris-daniel]]** (Krzysztof Daniel), who maintains WardleyMaps.com and there hosts &
expands Wardley's CC-licensed book — now captured as the Wardley primary
([[wardley-maps-value-chains-and-evolution]]). So the method side of this pairing is grounded.

**Video observations (part 1) added** (raw note `raw/notes/daniel-em-wardley-video-observations.md`). A verbatim transcript
still couldn't be pulled (YouTube's caption backend wouldn't load even via live Chrome), but reading the
frames directly upgraded this from a pure pointer to an **observed worked example**:

- Daniel works a **software consultancy's time-tracking product**. He first builds a **"Strategy Level
  Wardley Map"** — *"What's the core focus of our software consultancy business? As related to time
  tracking"* — placing subsystems on a value-chain × **maturity/evolution** axis to reason about
  **build vs buy / where to focus**.
- He then renders the chosen subsystems as an **Event Model** with capability-like **swimlanes** —
  *Time Management, User registration, Client management, Project management* — in the standard
  command→event→read-model vocabulary.
- Channel's own framing: the two are "complementary practices that allow an organization to make a
  concrete and deterministic system that is guided with strategy."

This is a concrete instance of the thesis and a nice illustration of the [[business-capabilities]] ×
[[wardley-mapping]] × [[event-modeling]] trio: strategy picks the capabilities worth owning; the event
model builds them.
