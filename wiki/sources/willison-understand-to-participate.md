# Willison — Understand to participate

Source: [[simon-willison]], *"Understand to participate"*, simonwillison.net, **2026-07-02** (tagged
coding-agents / cognitive-debt). Raw capture: `raw/articles/willison-understand-to-participate.md`. A
short note relaying a framing from **Geoffrey Litt**'s AI Engineer World's Fair 2026 talk.

## Summary

Willison amplifies a single phrase from Geoffrey Litt's AIE talk — **"understand to participate"** — on
the challenge of collaborating with coding agents as they build ever-larger changes without accumulating
**cognitive debt** as your understanding drifts from how the code actually works.

## Key points

- **You must understand the code deeply enough to *participate further* with the model.** Litt: "you need
  a rich set of concepts in your mind to think creatively and fluently about how to move something
  forward. If you're lacking that fluency, your ability to participate in the project is meaningfully
  limited."
- **Understanding is the guard against cognitive/comprehension debt** — not a release gate you clear once,
  but the ongoing capacity to stay an *active participant* in the creative process rather than a passive
  approver of agent output.
- A short link-note (Willison's amplification), not a standalone thesis; Litt also posted a thread version
  of the talk.

## Why it matters

A compact, quotable anchor for the **comprehension-debt** seam that recurs across the focus area: it is the
same concern [[addyosmani-own-the-outer-loop|Osmani]] frames as **cognitive debt** (understanding drifts as
horizons lengthen; the Anthropic RCT's −17pp comprehension gap) and [[tornhill-ai-readable-code-series|Tornhill]]
frames from the code-health side, and it names the *why* behind [[ai-readable-code]] and
[[agent-legibility]] — keep the human able to participate, not just approve. "Understand to participate" is
the crisp verbal handle the KB lacked. Caveat: a second-hand relay of Litt's framing, no original argument.
