# Source: Gartner — 40% of Enterprise Apps Will Feature Task-Specific AI Agents by 2026

The **primary** [[gartner]] press release (2025-08-26, analyst Anushree Verma) behind the
most-cited agentic-AI forecast in the KB. Replaces the secondhand citation in
[[svitla-agentic-ai-market-trends-2026]] for these specific numbers. Raw capture:
`raw/articles/gartner-40-percent-enterprise-apps-task-specific-agents-2026.md`.

## Summary

Gartner frames agentic AI as a **five-stage evolution** from embedded assistants to
cross-application agent ecosystems, with the headline prediction that **up to 40% of
enterprise applications will include task-specific agents by 2026, up from <5%** in 2025.

## The five stages

1. **AI assistants for every application** (by end of 2025) — precursors that depend on human
   input; calling them "agents" is **[[agentwashing]]** (this is the primary origin of the term).
2. **Task-specific agent applications** (2026) — the 40% prediction; agents perform complex
   end-to-end tasks (e.g. a cybersecurity threat-response agent).
3. **Collaborative AI agents within an application** (2027) — one-third of agentic
   implementations will combine differently-skilled agents; needs agent-to-agent
   communication/interoperability standards.
4. **AI agent ecosystems across applications** (2028) — networks of specialized agents
   collaborating across apps and functions; a third of user experiences shift from native
   apps to **agentic front ends**.
5. **The "new normal"** (2029) — at least 50% of knowledge workers develop skills to work
   with, govern, or create agents on demand.
