# Source: Svitla — Agentic AI Market Trends 2025–2026

Trends article by Debra Garcia / Svitla Team, [[svitla-systems]], 2026-04-27. The broad
market-and-trends survey of the space, citing Gartner, IDC, Fortune Business Insights and
named case studies. Raw capture: `raw/articles/svitla-agentic-ai-market-trends-2026.md`.

## Summary

Agentic AI is scaling fast — market ~$7.6B (2025) → ~$10.8B (2026), with [[gartner]]
forecasting 40% of enterprise apps embedding task-specific agents by end of 2026 (up from
<5%). But **79% adoption vs 11% production** is the defining gap, and Gartner expects >40% of
agentic projects canceled by 2027 over cost, value, and governance.

## Key points

- **[[agentwashing]]:** most enterprise "agents" are really assistants (respond to prompts,
  human-directed) rather than true agents (plan, use tools, act with minimal oversight) —
  which is why adoption looks high but production stays low.
- **Proof points (crossing the gap):** IRS tax-court case openings 10 days → 30 min; a
  retailer's quarterly inventory losses $5.4M → $1.6M; AtlantiCare clinical assistant 80%
  adoption, documentation time −42% (~66 min/clinician/day); a Fortune 500 report cycle 15
  days → 35 min at $9 vs $2,200. Common thread: task-specific, high-volume, well-defined work.
- **Five 2026 trends:** (1) [[multi-agent-orchestration]] via [[model-context-protocol]]
  (agent→system) and [[agent2agent-protocol]] (agent→agent); (2) [[agentic-coding]] going
  mainstream; (3) [[guardian-agents]] as a governance layer; (4) [[agentic-commerce]];
  (5) low-code platforms democratizing who builds agents.
- **Drivers:** IDC sees AI spend $1.3T by 2029; [[salesforce-agentforce]] hit $1.4B ARR
  (with Data 360); 400+ agent startups mapped by CB Insights.
- **Friction:** cost escalation from PoC to production, unclear ROI, governance gaps, plus AI/ML
  talent scarcity (63% report shortages).
- **[[autonomy-ladder]]:** Chain → Workflow → Partially autonomous → Fully autonomous. Most
  2026 deployments sit at Level 1–2; marketing implies Level 3–4.
