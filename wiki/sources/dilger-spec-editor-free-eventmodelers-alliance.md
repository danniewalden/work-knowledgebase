# Martin Dilger — Spec Editor goes free; the Miro ↔ Eventmodelers "Alliance"

LinkedIn post by **[[martin-dilger]]** (2026-06-19; captured same day via logged-in Chrome).
Source file: `raw/notes/dilger-spec-editor-free-miro-eventmodelers-alliance.md`. A product/tooling
announcement, on-thread for the EM × agents focus.

## What it says

- Dilger's "north star" is for **Modeling to change the software industry** — not as another
  methodology but as the best way to **close requirement gaps** by getting people modeling together.
- He frames two tools as a deliberate **"Alliance"** rather than a migration:
  **Model in Miro → Enhance with AI → Generate Code → Scale to Enterprise.** The Miro Toolkit (where
  many business users already live) will keep being maintained; the [[eventmodelers-ai|Eventmodelers
  Platform]] is the "AI-enabled evolution" for handing a model to engineers where AI can enrich it.
- Concrete change: he is **making previously-commercial Miro Toolkit features free, starting with the
  Spec Editor.** Teams can now define **[[given-when-then|Given / When / Then]] scenarios per slice**
  and capture requirements directly in Miro "where the conversations happen" (he shows a "vertical
  specification" built in a workshop).

## Why it matters here

Minor as news (a pricing/feature move), but it reinforces two focus threads with a concrete artifact:
(1) **GWT-per-slice as the requirements unit** — the spec editor operationalizes the
[[given-when-then]] contract on each [[vertical-slice-architecture|slice]] inside a collaborative
modeling tool; (2) the **two-surface tooling story** for [[eventmodelers-ai]] — a familiar collaborative
canvas (Miro) feeding an AI-enabled platform that enriches the model and drives [[agentic-coding|code
generation]]. It's consistent with Dilger's [[spec-driven-development]] thesis (the model/spec is the
environment that constrains the agent) and his "model is a living spec" pipeline
([[dilger-model-is-a-living-spec-always-on-agent]], [[dilger-event-modeling-agent-harness]]).

## Caveats

Vendor self-report; no independent review, and the substantive nugget (free Spec Editor + GWT capture)
sits inside marketing framing. "Alliance" is Dilger's branding for keeping the Miro toolkit alongside
the web platform.
