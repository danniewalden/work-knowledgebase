Automatic Domain Discovery using Claude Code.

You've rarely look at your own product the way a brand-new user does - clicking through every flow, hitting every weird edge case. But what if something could do exactly that, automatically, and show you the result as a visual storyboard?

Point an AI agent at your systems UI and tell it to map out the flows. It doesn't read your code or your docs - it looks at your actual screens, the way a real user would. And it builds a visual timeline of how your product actually works, not how you think it works.

Chances are, it'll find things you didn't know were there. Confusing navigation paths, flows that don't quite make sense, screens that contradict each other. Not because your product is bad - but because nobody's looked at it this way before. It's a mirror, and mirrors aren't always flattering. But they're useful.

Usually I ask clients to prepare for a workshop by sketching out some UI flows in advance - this can take hours.

- hours spent taking screenshots
- documenting flows
- trying to capture "how things actually work" before anyone can even start improving them.

It saves time in the workshop, but it´s boring typically.

This entire process - discovering flows, taking screenshots, building the visual storyboard - can now happen automatically, on autopilot in the Event Modelers Plattform. By the time you sit down to actually work on your system, you already have a real, honest starting point.

Domain Discovery doesn't have to mean writing boring documentation.

Sometimes it just means letting an agent show you what's really there in your product - including the things you didn't know were broken.

And the amazing thing - building the "discovery" took exactly 15min. It´s just another skill. You can extend this to your need, let it comment on screens, find flaws in the UX..
