I have a lot of ideas about the future of software development, but today at PayFit we had the chance to meet someone who is building it - Stephane Jourdan from Anyshift.io.

AI agents grepping around systems is a poor substitute for having graphs of your system that can be queried to provide current and historical states of the system, either for diagnosing production issues or anticipating the blast radius of a change.

Graphs + Memory + Skills + Agents = It does seem clear at this point that these are the key building blocks we are all going to be using.

It's fantastic to meet people who are bringing this to life.
