What if 35–45% of your agent token spend is wasted?

Most enterprises are now on a trajectory toward agentic AI. The general assumption is simple: faster
coding will translate into increased productivity.

Unfortunately, our research suggests that agents may perform worst where they are needed most, in the
parts of legacy codebases with the highest technical debt. Most industrial codebases are not ready for
the agentic era.

And even if agents could operate effectively in such code, our recent research shows that unhealthy
code increases token spending by 35%-45% across Java, C++, and Python.

The question is, how do we avoid this?

Join us June 26 at 7 pm CET for a live session "What Unhealthy Code Costs in the Agentic Era and How
to Fix It" with Dr. Markus Borg and Asko Nõmm.

Markus will walk you through our latest research, and Asko will share the agentic workflow his team
uses at CodeScene to keep generated code healthy from the start.
