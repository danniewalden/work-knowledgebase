Best Practice - Highlighting in Event Modeling

I teach that for years in my Event Modeling courses.

Don´t fear complex screens, because most screens are unfortunately.
But at any point in time in the system, typically only one part of the screen is interesting.

Mark it. Make it explicit what is important right now.
Not in 5 min, not 5 min ago, right now.

Nowadays I typically use HTML Screens, oftentimes directly using the Design Systems provided. Just much faster to generate than sketching by hand.

This is not always the right choice though. Especially in the beginning, ugly screens are better. More focus, less distraction. Beautiful comes later.

On the Eventmodelers-Platform you can now add markers, just by selecting anything on a screen, dynamically blurring anything outside the markers if you want to.
I liked it immediately.

But again something I didn't think of.. Those markers give context to agents.

They can read them,
they can understand them.
They can validate them and use them to build the UI.
