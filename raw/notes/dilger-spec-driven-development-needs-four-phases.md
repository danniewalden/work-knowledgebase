your spec-driven development workflow (sdd) falters in production.

Spec-Driven Development needs 4 phases.

1) Idea -> Intent
2) Intent -> Spec
3) Spec -> Code
4) Code -> Operations & Maintenance

Most frameworks and handbooks like Kiro, SpecKit and also Anthrophics latest Handbook on Software SDLC - focus almost exclusively on 3)

Handwaving 1),2) and 4) with a few sentences.

Leaving out 75% of the work, that needs to be done.

that's why you constantly struggle.

Just writing better markdown files won't help, and neither do more and stricter reviews.

1) and 4) are the tough ones.
2) is mechanical if 1) is done right
3) the easiest of them all

You are not doing SDD if you don't have an answer to 1) or 4)
