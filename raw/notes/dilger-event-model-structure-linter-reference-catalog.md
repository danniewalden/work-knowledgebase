Rating the structure of an Event Model based on heuristics - how interesting!

William Power came up with an interesting idea today and I think I will explore this.

He had some initial Event Models created and wanted to know whether they are structurally sound.

He asked me whether I had any Boards publicly available, which I consider "well structured".
Of course I do! That´s what the catalog is for, so I pointed him there.

He then used Claude Code to compare his Boards against the "Reference", just to get an understanding whether the structure is similar.

What a neat idea!

This doesn´t tell you whether your proesses are modelled correctly, but it tells you at least you structured them correctly.
Like a 'linter' for Event Modeling.

I´m thinking about putting this into the Plattform as a feature. Would you use this?
