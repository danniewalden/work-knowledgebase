Mastering TODO Lists in Event Modeling was hard.

Looking back, I don´t really remember why. It´s quite simple, just a queue of things to do essentially.

The "trick" is how to describe the behavior in a way it´s easy to understand by human and AI alike.

Traditionally we used GWT ( given / when / then ) you may know from gherkin or any other behavior-driven approach. Nothing new.

Instead of writing several scenarios for this - oftentimes it´s way easier to just use one scenario to describe it all. Much more compact.

I used to name them "Vertical Specs", as they are typically laid out vertically below a slice. But this confused people, so now I just refer to them as Storylines.

Examples that tell a story. And they are available since today to use. Give it a try.
