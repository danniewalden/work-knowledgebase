Adding Perspectives to Event Modeling - something that simply wasn't possible before.

It's a feature I've wanted in Miro since I first started using Event Modeling.

Not every audience needs the same level of detail.

Different stakeholders have different questions:
- C-Level wants the big picture. How many slices are there? What's the overall scope? When will it be done?
- Engineers need the full story. Business rules, edge cases, workflows, and all the details that matter during implementation.
- Architects often want a higher-level view as well, but focused on components, boundaries, integrations, and system structure.
- Stakeholders - mainly interested in the narrative and business process.

For now, we've introduced our first perspective: the High-Level View.

It strips away the noise and lets you focus on the overall flow of the system.
But there is much more we could do.

An Architect Perspective could be particularly interesting. Imagine deriving architecture views directly from an Event Model.
Maybe even generating something like C4 from the model automatically?
I'm curious: what perspectives would you add?
