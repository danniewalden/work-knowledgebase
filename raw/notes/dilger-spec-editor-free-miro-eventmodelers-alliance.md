I want Modeling to change the software industry. That´s my north star.

Not because it's another methodology, but because it makes building software smoother, clearer, and far less frustrating for everyone involved.

Over the years, we've used Modeling in Miro with countless clients. And one thing became obvious:
There is no better way to close requirement gaps than getting people together and modeling.

The Eventmodelers Platform is the natural next step - an AI-enabled evolution that takes modeling beyond Miro into a dedicated web-based environment.
For many people, that feels easier.

But not for everyone.

For a lot of business people, Miro is their daily bread and butter. They know it inside out. It's already part of their workflow. It's familiar, established, and trusted.
That's where many teams start.

And that's exactly why the Miro Toolkit is not being abandoned. Quite the opposite - it will continue to be actively maintained and improved.
I don't want to force anyone into a new tool or platform.

Use what already works for you.

Model in Miro. Collaborate with your team. Then hand over your model to your engineers in the Eventmodelers Platform, where AI can help enrich the model, add details, and accelerate delivery.

Together, these tools form a natural Alliance:
Model in Miro → Enhance with AI → Generate Code → Scale to Enterprise

From day one, modeling inside the Eventmodelers Platform has been completely free.
And because I want the modeling experience to be as smooth and accessible as possible, I'm starting to make previously commercial features from the Miro Toolkit available to everyone for free.

Starting today with the Spec Editor.
(In the screenshot, you can see a vertical specification we created during yesterday's workshop.)

You can now freely define Given / When / Then scenarios for your slices and capture requirements directly where the conversations happen.

I hope it helps teams create better software together.
