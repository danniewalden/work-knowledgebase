I see tons of people building harnesses for AI assisted development.. same mistake we engineers always do..

Throwing technology at problems that can't be solved with technology alone.

Don´t get me wrong, I´m not saying those people don´t know what they are doing. Most of them are amazing. But the harness.. and the code.. is just 20%.. of the solution.

This leaves us with 80% of ... what?

Understanding what to do with the harness and the code. Understanding what problem are we trying solving with it.

If your requirements are unclear, blurry, crappy and full of gaps - no matter how many agents, roles, skills and guardrails you define in your agent harness - the result will be mediocre at best.

It´s like going to cut down those trees with a freshly bought chainsaw.. but nobody told you which trees to cut..

I see tons of posts for the last 20%, basically none for the first 80%.

Clarifying requirements, understanding business processes and how to bring them in a format that works.

"I spent weeks crafting this beautiful harness, with agents, skills, guardrails .. just specify your requirements as markdown and go.."

But nobody tells you how to write that damn markdown. How much detail is necessary? THIS has always been the hard part, there is no technical solution to this. It's a human problem, a communications problem..

I´m focusing most of my work on the first 80%.. and it turns out the rest becomes pretty easy if the problem is cleared.

But of course building the harness, taming the agent is way more fun.. that´s the problem.
