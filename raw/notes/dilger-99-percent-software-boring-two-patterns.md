99% of Software is boring.

That's not a complaint, it's a fact. Just took me 20 years to learn.
We've been rebuilding the same systems over and over - before AI and after AI. Only the tools changed.

Every system, no matter how "unique" it looks on the surface, breaks down into two repeating patterns:
State change - how information gets into the system
State view - how information gets used, and pulled back out

I usually tell workshop groups there are four patterns, just to not be too boring. But between us - it´s only two.

I´m using Event Modeling for 5 years now to model Systems.
It's always the same thing.

We engineers love to think of ourselves as creative thinkers, solving novel problems. In the end, we're putting together Lego bricks - and we effectively only use two colors to do it.

Once you accept that it's only two patterns, AI becomes dramatically more useful.

No need to hand-write the repetitive state-change / state-view code. It´s nothing but glorified copy & paste.

The repetitive nature of event modeling is exactly what makes it a great fit for AI - it gives AI tight guardrails and clear instructions instead of an open-ended blank page

After this weeks webinar someone wrote me with a sharp observation. We were talking about the combination of Event Modeling and Spec-Driven Development Frameworks like Githubs Spec Kit.

The question was simple - "I don´t get it, with everything you showed us - why do we even need all of that?"

This person got it.

There is a truly creative part in every software project. It just has nothing to do with code. It´s navigating company politics.

Most companies have policies - "we are doing spec driven development with Spec Kit". Even if it doesn´t add much to the game, you can´t just skip this.
So we meet companies where they are.

Event Modeling alone is a perfect tool for Spec Driven Development - but it´s easily combined with Spec-Kit, Kiro, Spec-Kitty and all the others, and I´m working on this right now.

If this is what is needed to help Teams adopt the process - let´s go.
