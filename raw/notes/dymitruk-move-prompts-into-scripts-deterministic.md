Move as much from your prompts and agent md files into scripts. Deterministic behaviour is your goal. Evidence of how things work should be intermediate text files in directories that correspond to steps in your processes - even inboxes and outboxes. You'll naturally arrive at #EventModeling and #EventSourcing.
