## Observed structure of the worked example (observation, not verbatim)

Daniel maps a **software consultancy's time-tracking product**: first a **Wardley map** to locate each
subsystem on the value-chain × evolution plane and reason about **build vs buy / focus** (strategy),
then an **Event Model** of the chosen subsystems with capability-like swimlanes (Time Management, User
registration, Client management, Project management) rendered in the standard command→event→read-model
vocabulary. This is a concrete instance of the series thesis: **Wardley = where to go and why (strategy,
build-vs-buy); Event Modeling = what to build (the deterministic design).**
