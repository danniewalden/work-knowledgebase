DCB is event sourcing what it always should have been..

Quite a few people complain about the complex and technical naming. "Dynamic Consistency Boundaries".. not exactly a marketing term.

And I agree.. actually we already had a good name for it. "Event Sourcing".

We should have found a name for the aggregate-based approach that so many people struggle with.

"Static Consistency Boundaries" maybe?

So what do you want to work with - "Static Consistency Boundaries" or "Event Sourcing"?

Much better 🤩

DCB gets a very prominent place in the second edition of "Understanding Eventsourcing" btw.
