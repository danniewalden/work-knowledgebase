Making Event Modeling an organizational success. Simple, definitely not easy.

"But Martin, you always tell us Event Modeling is so simple!"

And it is. I can explain Event Modeling in five minutes. You'll get it right away.

For a long time I thought that was the hard part. Get the notation right, get the sticky notes moving, and the rest follows.

It doesn't. The "problem" was never the methodology. It's everything that comes with it.

Making Modeling real means roles change, the organizational setup shifts, the way of working is never the same again.

This is one of the biggest levers a company has for AI Integration. None of it comes for free.

I used to think teaching it well was the whole job.

Wrong. Event Modeling isn't a training problem. It's a gigantic shift left on the organization. You solve the hard problems while they're still cheap, not months later when the assumptions are buried in code.

Not all teams are ready, and it never happens on day one. Start small, one step at a time. We've done this with dozens of teams, and most tell me the same thing: they never want to go back.

I just read an article describing the "new way of working". Mentioning "Collaborative Design Sessions" as one point amongst many. As if inviting everyone to a meeting makes them work together. Those people have never worked in a company it seems.

What you need is:

- clear methodology to drive focused Workshops ( 1x/2x 30min a week is enough )
- tooling (don´t force teams onto whiteboards, we spent months building tooling that saves hours a day per engineer)
- training & facilitation (skip the trial and error we already solved years ago)
- organizational buy-in (start bottom-up, but you'll need it eventually, and we have the numbers to help)

For some teams, Event Modeling stays a few sticky notes on a wall. For others, it's the red pill.
